PLATE 2

The money map — five legs, two hidden ones, three modes

The platform controls four of the five legs; leg 3 belongs to the debtor. Hybrid mode makes the platform the converter — legs 2a and 3a move value between its own treasuries.

fiat rail — hours to days, reversible for a period stablecoin rail — seconds, final at confirmation conversion legs 2a / 3a — the platform is the converter (hybrid)
PLATE 3

Assurance tiers — what each attestor rules out

Fraud, in evidence terms, is a claim attested only by the supplier. Each tier adds an independent attestor; the tier sets eligibility and advance rate through the RPA's schedule — never the rate.
